Rooted in Change

One account, four roots

Every part of Paliriza branches from the same idea — see your impact clearly, then act on it.

Your own account

A short lifestyle questionnaire on sign-up — transport, diet, energy, waste, shopping, even how often you go out — builds your starting footprint from day one.

Daily tracking

A quick daily check-in updates your footprint and feeds a running history, so trends show up over weeks, not just a single snapshot.

Points, streaks & badges

Lower-carbon choices earn more points. Logging consistently builds a streak. Milestones unlock badges — habit-building with a bit of momentum behind it.

Ask anything

A built-in AI companion answers real questions about sustainability, tied back to the same context as your footprint — not a generic chatbot bolted on the side.
